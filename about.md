  <h1>White Ravens</h1>
  <p>White Ravens is a small, open-source, self-hosting, privacy-first non-profit collective. We build tools and communities that put people back in control of their data &mdash; and we publish almost everything we make.</p>

## What we do

We design and run a handful of services for ourselves and our friends, and open-source the pieces that are useful to anyone else. Most of our work lives in three places:

- **The collective** — long-form pages, governance, and the people behind it &mdash; at <a href="https://whiteravens.net">whiteravens.net</a>.
- **The services** — documentation, status, and self-hosting guides at <a href="https://wrservices.link">wrservices.link</a>.
- **The code** — every repository under <a href="https://github.com/whiteravens20">github.com/whiteravens20</a>, MIT-licensed unless explicitly marked otherwise.

This blog is where we share engineering notes, release write-ups, post-mortems when things break, and the occasional opinion piece.

## What we believe

- **Open by default.** If a thing can be public, it should be. The interesting bits aren't the secrets &mdash; they're the rationale.
- **Self-hosted first.** We run what we build, on hardware we control, before recommending it to anyone else.
- **Privacy is a feature, not a feature flag.** No third-party trackers, no shadow analytics, no font CDNs phoning home from this blog.
- **Small over scalable.** We'd rather ship something honest for a hundred people than something cynical for a million.
